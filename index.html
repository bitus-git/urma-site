Materials and testing

Designed and printed in Estonia. Backed by science.

We print every product in Estonia using certified materials that are safe to handle — children included. And we test what we make alongside clinical psychologists and their patients, so the promise holds up: the right object for the mood you’re trying to reach.

Settle material
TPU 95A
Flexible thermoplastic polyurethane. Oil-resistant, doesn't crack, survives a washing machine.
Play material
PLA
Plant-derived, rigid, holds a crisp mechanism. Recyclable through our take-back box.
Layer height
0.15 mm
Fine enough that gears mesh and edges feel finished, not printed.
