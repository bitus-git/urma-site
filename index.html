Why fidget

Hands want a job.

Moving a little is how a lot of people think, wait and cope. A good fidget gives that movement a place to go.

Fidgets are tools, not treatments. What works is personal, and that is why we want you to try them.

Focus

Busy hands, quieter head.

Many people concentrate better with a small repeated movement running in the background. It takes the edge off restlessness so attention can stay on the page, the call or the problem.

Calm

Something steady to hold when it gets loud.

Slow, repeated pressure and texture give hands an anchor during stress, long waits and hard conversations. Squeeze, stretch, let go, repeat.

Habits

Better than the pen click.

Chewed caps, picked nails and endless scrolling are all hands looking for something to do. A fidget gives the habit a quieter, kinder home.

Sensory needs

Different hands, different needs.

Some people want soft and silent. Others want crisp and clicky. Many neurodivergent people already use fidgets and stim tools, and the right feel differs from person to person. That is why we make two lines.

Anywhere

Pocket-sized and polite.

Settle makes no sound at all, so it fits in a classroom, a meeting or a train carriage. Play is for the desk and the sofa, where a click is part of the fun.

Two moods, two ranges

Your mind doesn’t always want the same thing.

Sometimes you need to cool off and decompress. Sometimes you need to think, play and get something finished. Either way your hands want a job, so we make one for each mood.

Materials and testing

Designed and printed in Estonia. Materials certified.

We print every object in Estonia using certified materials that are safe to handle. We test what we make with the people who use it, in classrooms, in clinics and in our own houses, and we change the design until it holds up.

Settle material
TPU 90–98A
Flexible thermoplastic polyurethane. Oil-resistant, doesn’t crack, survives a washing machine.
Play material
PLA
Plant-derived, rigid, holds a crisp mechanism straight off the printer.
Layer height
0.15 mm
Fine enough that gears mesh and edges feel finished, not printed.
