Materials and testing

Designed and printed in Estonia. Materials certified.

We print every product in Estonia using certified materials that are safe to handle. We test what we make with the people who use them — in classrooms, in clinics, and in our own houses — and we change the design until it holds up.

See the materials
Settle material
TPU 90–98A
Flexible thermoplastic polyurethane. Oil-resistant, doesn’t crack, survives a washing machine.
Play material
PLA
Plant-derived, rigid, holds a crisp mechanism straight off the printer.

0.15 mm
